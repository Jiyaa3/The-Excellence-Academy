Class 8 to 10 Foundation Coaching

Building rock-solid concepts in Mathematics, Science and English for GSEB, CBSE & ICSE students. We transform rote learners into conceptual problem solvers ready for Class 10 Board exams and future competitive streams.

Eligible Classes Class 8, 9 & 10 (Separate Batches)
Core Subjects Maths, Science, English, SST
Timings Mon to Sat: 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Batch Limit 20–25 Students per Batch (Strict Cap)

About The Foundation Course

Classes 8, 9 and 10 form the cornerstone of every student's academic future. A shallow understanding here creates hurdles later in 11th Science, Commerce and competitive exams. At The Excellence Academy, we teach from first principles.

Mathematics Mastery

  • Algebra, Linear Equations, Quadratic Formula & Polynomials
  • Geometry theorems, Triangles & Coordinate Geometry
  • Trigonometry fundamentals & Heights and Distances
  • Statistics, Probability & Surface Areas
  • RD Sharma & Exemplar high-order thinking problems (HOTS)

Science (Phy, Chem, Bio)

  • Physics: Motion, Force, Gravitation, Electricity, Light & Optics
  • Chemistry: Chemical Reactions, Acids & Bases, Metals & Non-metals, Carbon Compounds
  • Biology: Life Processes, Control & Coordination, Heredity & Environment
  • Experimental demonstrations and diagram-drawing techniques

English & Social Science

  • Grammar fundamentals, Comprehension & Formal writing
  • History: National Movement, Industrialization & World Events
  • Geography: Resources, Agriculture & Map Work practice
  • Civics & Economics: Democratic politics & Economic development
  • Speed-writing and point-wise presentation for 5-mark questions

What Every Enrolled Student Receives

  • Strict Small Batches (20–25 Students): The teacher can detect immediately when a student is stuck on a numerical or theorem.
  • Weekly Written Tests: Every Sunday test simulates the official Board question paper pattern (1, 2, 3 and 5 mark questions).
  • Chapter-wise Practice Booklets: Curated question banks containing previous 10 years board questions with model solutions.
  • Monthly Parent Review: Detailed report cards highlighting attendance, test score trajectories and teacher observations.
