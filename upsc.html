UPSC Civil Services (IAS / IPS / IFS)

A disciplined, mentor-led coaching program in Gandhinagar covering Prelims General Studies, CSAT Paper 2, Mains GS 1 to 4, Essay writing drills and Interview personality enhancement.

Target Stages Prelims + Mains + Interview
Course Duration 1-Year & 2-Year Foundation
Batch Options Morning, Evening & Weekend
Class Size 25–30 Students (Strict Cap)

Curriculum & Exam Strategy

Cracking the UPSC Civil Services Examination requires a structured methodology: conceptual clarity, rigorous static revision, daily analytical reading of newspapers and extensive answer writing practice.

Prelims GS & CSAT

  • Indian Polity & Constitution (M. Laxmikanth deep dive)
  • Modern, Ancient & Medieval Indian History & Art
  • Physical, Human & Economic Geography
  • Macroeconomics, Fiscal Policy & Economic Survey
  • Ecology, Biodiversity & Climate Change
  • General Science & Contemporary Technology
  • CSAT Paper 2: Quantitative Aptitude & Reading Comprehension

Mains Papers (GS 1 to 4)

  • GS 1: Heritage, Culture, World History & Society
  • GS 2: Governance, Constitution, Social Justice & IR
  • GS 3: Economic Dev, Agriculture, Internal Security & Disaster Mgmt
  • GS 4: Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude & Case Studies
  • Essay Paper structuring, thesis framing & argumentation
  • Compulsory Indian Language & English papers

Mentorship & Test Series

  • Daily 2-Question Mains Answer Writing Practice
  • Weekly OMR-based 100-Question Prelims Mock Tests
  • Personalized copy checking with constructive feedback
  • Daily editorial breakdown from The Hindu & Indian Express
  • Monthly Current Affairs compilations & Yojana summaries
  • Mock Interviews with retired bureaucrats & subject experts

The Excellence Academy Advantage for UPSC

  • Gandhinagar Location: Quiet, academic capital ambiance with access to library reading rooms and serious peer groups.
  • Strict Batch Limit (25–30 Students): Maximum 25–30 students per batch (strict cap) ensures one-on-one answer critique and individualized progress tracking.
  • Balanced Static + Dynamic: Core concepts are grounded in NCERTs, while current affairs are mapped directly to UPSC syllabus keywords.
  • Answer Writing from Month 1: We do not postpone answer writing until after Prelims. Aspirants master introductions, flowcharts and conclusions early.
